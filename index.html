EchoLight | ElleHacks

EchoLight is an accessibility-focused application my team and I developed during ElleHacks 2026 to help deaf and hard-of-hearing users stay aware of important sounds in their environment. It detects sounds like emergency sirens and fire alarms in real time and translates them into visual alerts on a web interface. I worked on the Python audio detection system and backend integration, helping turn our idea into a working proof of concept within the hackathon's time constraints. I really enjoyed combining creative problem-solving with technology to build something that could make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

LUME Projects | Responsive Business Website

A responsive website I'm developing for LUME Projects Inc., a company offering business consulting and decluttering services. Since the two services are quite different, I designed a split-screen homepage that allows visitors to easily navigate to the section relevant to them. The layout adapts to different screen sizes, switching from a horizontal split on desktop to vertically stacked sections on mobile. I'm building the site from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on intuitive navigation and a clean, accessible design.
